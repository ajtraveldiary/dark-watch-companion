About this spoiler-free guide to Dark

Dark is a German Netflix series (2017–2020, three seasons, 26 episodes) set in the small town of Winden, where a child's disappearance pulls four families into a mystery. It has a large cast and a story that is famously hard to keep straight, so this site helps you follow it while you watch, without spoiling anything ahead.

Pick the last episode you finished. Everything on the page, including the relationship tree, timeline, character notes, open mysteries, glossary and recaps, then shows only what the show has revealed up to that episode. Mark the next episode and it updates, highlighting what's new. Twists are shown the way the show presents them until the episode that reveals the truth.

Questions

Is it really spoiler-free?

Yes, as far as the episode you've marked. Nothing from later episodes is shown, not even their titles. If you go back to an earlier episode, the page goes back with you.

What can I use it for while watching?

Checking who someone is and how they're related, catching up on what happened in the last episode before starting the next one, and keeping track of the open questions the show has raised so far.

Do I need an account?

No. It's free, with no ads and no sign-up. Your progress is saved only in your own browser.

Is this made by Netflix?

No. It's an unofficial fan project and isn't affiliated with Netflix.
